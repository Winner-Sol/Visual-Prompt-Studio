|***What stayed the same?(Medium, values, ideas, subject matter)***|
Something that has stayed consistent throughout all three artwork is a means to ***express an idea to the viewers.***|In Persistence of Memory, Dali wishes to express that time can be manipulated based on your perspective, but what can’t be manipulated is the inevitable decay of time. In the Funerary mask of Tutankhamun, the craftsmen wish to protect the Pharaoh's soul and express the divine-like being that he had become in the afterlife. In the packaging video, the Youtuber wants to express that they prioritize organization and customer satisfaction.
