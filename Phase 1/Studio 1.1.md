**Assignment title:**Visual Prompt Studio Phase 1.1
**Prompt:**Create a collage on a sign in front of a supermarket designed as a form of social protest against over consumerism. The main message is to raise awareness about how damaging it can be to invest so much into short lived wants over needs. The subject matter includes burning money, ecosystems being depleted, and a balance scale to represent wants more than needs. The style should be abstract with bleeding colors to symbolize short lived wants that are tainting needs.
**Vocabulary Breakdown:**Medium-Materials and techniques used to convey a message or concept through art
Meaning- a concept conveyed through art, expression, words,etc
Subject matter- Elements that make up or support the subject.
Abstract Art- A style of art that uses shapes,colors,texture, lines, etc to depict emotions,feelings, or conceptual meaning rather than accurate depictions of reality.
